# glTF Models

Here are the best places to find and download a free, coarse wind turbine 3D model directly in **glTF** (`.gltf` or `.glb`) format.

### 1. Poly.Pizza (Best for "Coarse" Low-Poly Models)

Poly Pizza is an archive of low-poly 3D models (many originally from Google Poly). Almost all of their models are available under a Creative Commons (CC-BY) license and download directly as `.glb` (the binary version of glTF) which is perfect for web development and simple rendering.

* **Search Link:** [poly.pizza/search/wind turbine](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://poly.pizza/search/wind%2520turbine)
* **What you'll find here:** Very simple, blocky models with exactly the components you mentioned (base, tower, generator house, 3 blades) without heavy textures.

### 2. Sketchfab

Sketchfab is the largest repository for web-based 3D models. When creators allow their models to be downloaded, Sketchfab automatically converts them into the `glTF` format.

* **Search Link:** [sketchfab.com/search?q=wind+turbine&type=models&features=downloadable]()
* **How to use:** Ensure the **"Downloadable"** toggle is checked. Look for models tagged as "Low Poly." When you click "Download 3D Model," it will explicitly offer you an auto-converted glTF file. Many are free under Creative Commons licenses.

### 3. CGTrader

CGTrader hosts professional models but has a massive "Free" section. They have excellent filters for finding specific file extensions.

* **Search Link:** [cgtrader.com/free-3d-models/wind-turbine]()
* **How to use:** In the filter dropdowns at the top, select **Formats > glTF**. You will find dozens of free wind turbines ready to download.

### 4. Kenney.nl (Game Assets)

Kenney provides completely public domain (CC0) game assets. While you won't find a standalone "Wind Turbine" category, his **"Nature Kit"** or **"City Kit"** often include simple, modular wind turbines.

* **Website:** [kenney.nl/assets]()
* **How to use:** His 3D kits include a `GLTF` folder in the download containing every single prop individually exported as `.gltf` or `.glb`.

### Note on `.glb` vs `.gltf`

When you download from these sites, you will often get a `.glb` file instead of `.gltf`.

* **`.gltf`** is a JSON text file accompanied by a separate `.bin` file and separate image textures.
* **`.glb`** is the exact same model, but everything (the JSON, the binary data, and the textures) is packed into one single file.

If your software asks for a `glTF`, it will almost universally accept a `.glb` file!
